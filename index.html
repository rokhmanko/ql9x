QL9X

QL9X is a private, single-user integration that lets the owner's personal assistant software, running on the owner's own computers, work with the owner's own Google account: read and organise Gmail, manage Calendar events, and work with Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts on the owner's behalf.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users. Contact: rokhmanko@gmail.com.
